What is Connected TV?

Streaming TV advertising, made simple.

Connected TV (CTV) is advertising inside the streaming apps people watch on their smart TVs — Prime Video, Hulu, Roku, ESPN and more. Instead of cable, your full-screen, non-skippable :30 spot plays right in the shows your customers stream every night — and you only pay for completed views.
